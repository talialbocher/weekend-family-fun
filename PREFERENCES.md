# Preferences

Standing preferences for the weekend itinerary. **Edit this file directly** — the
routine reads it fresh every week, so changes here take effect on the next run
without touching the routine itself.

## Who this is for

A family of three: two adults and a **10-year-old son**.

The important nuance: **he is into whatever we are into.** Do not plan "kid
activities." No playground-and-splash-pad days, no chain restaurants with a
crayon menu, no children's museums as the main event. He'll do a real 3-mile
trail, a real museum, and a real restaurant.

What his age *does* mean in practice:
- Keep single stretches of walking under ~4 miles, and hikes at moderate grade
  or easier — distance is fine, technical scrambles are not.
- Avoid things that are structurally adult-only: bar crawls, wine tastings as
  the main activity, long prix-fixe dinners, 21+ venues.
- Avoid plans built around waiting in a long line (he'll be bored, not charmed).
- Restaurants should be places where a kid at the table is unremarkable —
  which is most good casual restaurants, not a constraint on cuisine.
