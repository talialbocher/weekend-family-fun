# Weekend Itinerary — August 22 to August 23

Weather: Saturday — showers early, clearing to partly cloudy, high 84°F (60% rain chance). Sunday — patchy morning fog, then mostly sunny, high 88°F.

## SATURDAY (Aug 22) — Staten Island day, built around the rain

Rain risk is real, so today stays compact around St. George, Staten Island, with an indoor-friendly anchor.

- 10:00 AM — Staten Island Ferry, St. George terminal. Free 25-min ride across the harbor past the Statue of Liberty and Ellis Island — covered cabin if it's pouring. Staten Island, NY. Drive: ~45 min to the ferry parking/terminal.
- 10:45 AM — St. George Waterfront Esplanade. Short waterfront stroll past the ballpark and harbor views; skip or shorten if rain is heavy. Staten Island, NY. Same area, no additional drive.
- 11:30 AM — Snug Harbor Cultural Center & Botanical Garden. 83-acre former sailors' home with botanical gardens plus indoor galleries (Staten Island Museum, Newhouse Center for Contemporary Art) — the rain hedge for the day. Staten Island, NY. ~5 min from the esplanade.
- 1:00 PM — Lunch: Frankies Sputino, St. George, Staten Island, NY — casual Tuscan-inspired Italian, comfortable sit-down setting.
- 2:15 PM — Dessert: Ralph's Famous Italian Ices, Staten Island, NY — SI institution since 1928, dozens of ice flavors.

~$165 today — the ferry is free but Snug Harbor's museum admission, lunch, and the Goethals Bridge toll add up; worth it for a first-time borough and a genuine rain hedge.

## SUNDAY (Aug 23) — Great Swamp hike + Clinton, NJ

Sunny and warmer, so the outdoor stretch moves to the morning.

- 9:30 AM — Great Swamp National Wildlife Refuge, Wildlife Observation Center boardwalk trails, Basking Ridge/Chatham, NJ. ~2 miles of flat, shaded boardwalk through marsh and forest — easy pace, good birdwatching. Drive: ~25 min.
- 11:30 AM — Drive to Clinton, NJ (~40 min from Great Swamp).
- 12:15 PM — Red Mill Museum Village, Clinton, NJ. Iconic red 1810 gristmill on the river, 12 historic buildings to wander — blacksmith shop, log cabin, one-room schoolhouse. On arrival.
- 1:30 PM — Lunch: Towne Restaurant, Clinton, NJ — family-owned American/Greek diner-style spot, downtown since 1979.
- 2:30 PM — Historic Main Street stroll, Clinton, NJ — walkable downtown along the South Branch Raritan River, shops and river views.
- 3:00 PM — Dessert: Clinton Creamery, Clinton, NJ — hand-dipped ice cream, malts, and sundaes on Main Street.

~$95 today — under budget.

## IF IT RAINS

- Saturday backup (if the morning shower runs long): stay indoors longer at Snug Harbor — Staten Island Museum and the Newhouse Center for Contemporary Art are both fully indoor — or swap in the National Lighthouse Museum, a 10-minute walk from the ferry terminal in St. George, NY.
- Sunday backup (if an unexpected system moves in): swap the Great Swamp boardwalk and Main Street stroll for the Hunterdon Art Museum, Clinton, NJ — contemporary art and craft housed in a restored 19th-century grist mill, right in the same town as lunch and dessert.
