Subject: Weekend Itinerary — November 14 to November 15

WEATHER
Saturday: Cool, patchy clouds with a chance of light morning rain clearing by afternoon, high near 47°F.
Sunday: Drier and a touch milder, partly sunny, high near 51°F.

We put the day with the rain risk (Saturday) somewhere forgiving and easy to duck indoors; Sunday, the nicer day, gets the hike.

SATURDAY — Asbury Park, NJ (~65 min from Millburn)

- ~11:00 AM — Boardwalk stroll: Asbury Park Boardwalk & Convention Hall (~65 min). The restored Beaux-Arts boardwalk, Convention Hall's arcade-era architecture, the Wonder Bar's giant Tillie mural. Flat, easy, fine even in a light drizzle.
- ~12:30 PM — Lunch: Iron Whale, Asbury Park (~65 min). Casual seafood right on the boardwalk, indoor seating.
- ~2:00 PM — Silverball Retro Arcade, Asbury Park (~65 min). Vintage pinball & arcade museum, unlimited play — a fun low-key extra that doubles as built-in rain cover.
- ~3:30 PM — Dessert: Cookman Creamery, Asbury Park (~65 min). Small-batch artisanal ice cream, uptown on Cookman Ave.

Rough cost: ~$150 for the day (lunch, arcade passes, ice cream, parking/gas) — on budget.

SUNDAY — Cross River, NY / Ridgefield, CT (~65–75 min from Millburn)

- ~10:00 AM — Hike: Ward Pound Ridge Reservation, Deer Hollow Trail Loop, Cross River, NY (~65 min). Moderate ~3.4-mile loop through Westchester's largest park — rock outcrops, stone walls, a creek crossing. New territory for us.
- ~12:00 PM — Aldrich Contemporary Art Museum, Ridgefield, CT (~75 min; ~15 min further than the trailhead). Rotating contemporary exhibits in a compact, walkable space — a short, kid-friendly visit.
- ~1:00 PM — Lunch: Bailey's Backyard, Ridgefield, CT (~75 min). Farm-to-table American, right on Main Street.
- ~2:30 PM — Dessert & stroll: Deborah Ann's Sweet Shoppe, Ridgefield, CT (~75 min), then a walk down Main Street — Connecticut's first designated "cultural district," walkable with boutiques and a historic feel.

Rough cost: ~$165 for the day; the extra is the Aldrich admission plus a proper sit-down lunch. Worth it — it's a whole new town for us, and the museum + Main Street stroll make the drive pay off.

IF IT RAINS

- Saturday is already rain-resilient as planned — if it's pouring, just spend more time at Silverball Retro Arcade and shorten the boardwalk walk; Iron Whale has full indoor seating.
- Sunday swap: if the trail's a washout, trade Ward Pound Ridge Reservation for a longer visit to the Aldrich Contemporary Art Museum plus the Keeler Tavern Museum & History Center, both in Ridgefield, CT — same town, same lunch and dessert plan holds.

New this week: Asbury Park Boardwalk, Convention Hall, Iron Whale, Silverball Retro Arcade, and Cookman Creamery (Asbury Park, NJ); Ward Pound Ridge Reservation (Cross River, NY); Aldrich Contemporary Art Museum, Bailey's Backyard, Deborah Ann's Sweet Shoppe, and the Keeler Tavern Museum (Ridgefield, CT) — nothing repeated from recent weeks, and both Asbury Park and Ridgefield are brand new to the rotation.
