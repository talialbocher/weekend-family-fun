Subject: Weekend Itinerary — September 5 to September 6

WEATHER
Saturday: Mostly cloudy, high 66°F — 40% chance of rain, mainly after 2pm. Morning is the dry window.
Sunday: Rain likely most of the day, high 64°F, breezy with gusts to ~35mph, 70% chance of precipitation (3/4"–1"). Built around staying indoors.

SATURDAY — Boonton, NJ (all one area, ~30 min from Millburn)
- 9:30am — Hike: Tourne County Park. Easy-moderate loop through boulder fields and forest to an overlook of the Rockaway Valley (aim for ~2–3 miles, e.g. the Tourne/Birchwood Lake loop). Drive: ~30 min.
- 11:30am — City stroll: Boonton Main Street Historic District — indie shops, a mill-town streetscape, and Grace Lord Park's waterfall overlook right off Main St. Drive: ~30 min.
- 12:30pm — Lunch: Fasil Mediterranean Restaurant, Boonton — casual, family-friendly Mediterranean fare. Drive: ~30 min.
- 1:30pm — Extra: Boonton's 135th Annual Labor Day Parade down Main St, followed by the Fire Dept. carnival (rides, games, food) at Boonton HS Field — free admission, runs into the evening. Good if the early-afternoon rain holds off; skip early if it doesn't. Drive: ~30 min.
- 2:30pm — Dessert: Curly's of Boonton — family-run, homemade hard ice cream and soft serve (121 Monroe St). Drive: ~30 min.
Rough cost: ~$120–130 (lunch, ice cream, optional carnival extras — parade/carnival admission is free).

SUNDAY — New Brunswick / Highland Park, NJ (~40–45 min from Millburn) — built indoors given the forecast
- 11:00am — Culture: Zimmerli Art Museum at Rutgers University, New Brunswick — real, substantial collection (Soviet nonconformist art, American/European painting), free admission. Drive: ~40 min.
- 12:30pm — Lunch: Tavern on George, New Brunswick — casual American, burgers and comfort food, on George St near campus. Drive: ~40 min.
- 2:00pm — Dessert: Sunmerry Bakery, Highland Park — pastries, breads, and macarons; a short hop across the river from New Brunswick. Drive: ~45 min.
Rough cost: ~$100–110.

IF IT RAINS EARLIER THAN EXPECTED (Saturday)
Swap the hike and parade for: Craftsman Farms (Stickley Museum), Parsippany, NJ — indoor guided tour of Gustav Stickley's Arts & Crafts-era log home, ~15 min from Boonton / ~25 min from Millburn. Boonton Main St also has covered shop-browsing if you just need to duck a shower between lunch and dessert.

New this week: Tourne County Park, Boonton Main Street, Fasil Mediterranean Restaurant, Curly's of Boonton, Boonton Labor Day Parade & Carnival, Craftsman Farms (Stickley Museum), Zimmerli Art Museum, Tavern on George, and Sunmerry Bakery — nothing repeated from the last several weeks, and no town reused from the last two weekends (Red Bank, Matawan, Beacon, Poughkeepsie/Highland, Staten Island, Basking Ridge/Chatham, Clinton).
