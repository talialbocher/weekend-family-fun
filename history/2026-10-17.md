Subject: Weekend Itinerary — October 17 to October 18

WEATHER
Saturday: Sunny and crisp, high near 52°F, breezy, cold start (upper 30s) — bundle up for the morning.
Sunday: Sunny and mild, high near 59°F, calmer — the nicer of the two days.

SATURDAY — Pine Barrens day trip (NJ)
This is a long drive (~85-90 min each way), worth it for the once-a-year Cranberry Festival.

- ~9:50 AM — Hike: Batsto Lake Trail (White Trail loop), Wharton State Forest, Hammonton, NJ. Easy, flat ~4-mile sandy loop around the lake, passing the old bog-iron works. Drive: ~85 min.
- ~11:30 AM — Stroll through Batsto Village: the 1800s ironworks/glassmaking village at the same trailhead — general store, gristmill, mansion. No extra drive.
- ~12:30 PM — Lunch: Lucille's Country Cooking, Warren Grove, NJ. Old-school Pine Barrens diner since 1975 — biscuits & gravy, blueberry pie. Drive: ~95 min.
- ~1:45 PM — Chatsworth Cranberry Festival, Chatsworth, NJ. The "unofficial capital of the Pine Barrens" hosts this juried craft show (150+ vendors) with live music — rain or shine. Drive: ~90 min.
- ~3:30 PM — Dessert: The Parlour, Chatsworth, NJ — handmade ice cream. Drive: ~90 min.

~$120/day — comfortably under budget despite the drive.

SUNDAY — Hudson Highlands (NY)
Another longer drive (~75-80 min), worth it for peak fall foliage week in the Highlands.

- ~10:20 AM — Hike: Appalachian Trail at Canopus Lake (north section), Clarence Fahnestock Memorial State Park, Kent, NY. Moderate, ~3.4-mile out-and-back to a viewpoint over the lake. Drive: ~80 min.
- ~12:00 PM — City stroll: Garrison Landing waterfront, plus the grounds of Boscobel House & Gardens (Maple Allée with Hudson River views), Garrison, NY. Drive: ~75 min.
- ~1:15 PM — Lunch: Hudson Hil's Market & Cafe, Cold Spring, NY — casual American, kids' menu. Drive: ~80 min.
- ~2:15 PM — Dessert: Angie's Cafe and Bakery, Cold Spring, NY — homemade baked goods since 1994 (chocolate chip cookies, blueberry muffins). Drive: ~80 min.

~$130/day — also under budget.

IF IT RAINS
- Saturday: the Cranberry Festival runs rain or shine, so it stays as planned. Swap the Batsto Lake loop for a walk through Batsto Village's historic buildings instead (general store, gristmill, mansion — mostly covered stops).
- Sunday: skip the Fahnestock hike and do the full indoor tour of Boscobel House itself (not just the grounds) in Garrison; keep lunch and dessert in Cold Spring as planned.

New this week: Wharton State Forest/Batsto Village, Lucille's Country Cooking, the Chatsworth Cranberry Festival, The Parlour, Fahnestock State Park, Garrison Landing & Boscobel House and Gardens, Hudson Hil's Market & Cafe, and Angie's Cafe and Bakery — nothing repeated from the ledger's last several months of weekends.
