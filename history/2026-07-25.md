Subject: Weekend Itinerary — July 25 to July 26

Weather: Saturday — sunny, high near 84°F, very low rain chance. Sunday — sunny, high near 83°F, low rain chance. A genuinely nice outdoor weekend, no rain plan needed but included below anyway.

Note: the ledger already had an entry for this exact weekend from an earlier run, but it broke the routine's own rules (it recommended South Mountain Reservation, which is avoid-listed, and a 4th repeat of the Hoboken waterfront walkway). This plan is built fresh, avoiding every place already in the ledger — including that flawed entry and the Aug 1-2 plan sent earlier — plus the permanent avoid list. That ruled out most of the close-in NJ/NYC spots already used up recently, so this one swings west to Chester, NJ and north to Nyack, NY.

SATURDAY — Chester, NJ (~45 min out) — approx. $80 for the 3 of you
- 10:00am — Hike: Hacklebarney State Park, Long Valley/Chester, NJ. Shaded trail along Trout Brook down to a scenic waterfall over the Black River, with bridges and rocks to scramble on the way — plus a small playground near the end as a reward. ~45 min from Millburn. (~$10 parking)
- 12:30pm — Lunch: Pren Kitchen, Chester, NJ — counter-service New American on Main St; scratch-made sandwiches, burgers, and soups. ~45 min from Millburn. (~$45)
- 2:00pm — Dessert: Sally Lunn's Tea Shoppe & Restaurant, Chester, NJ — scones and homemade sweets in a cozy Main St spot; good excuse for a stroll through Chester's antique shops after. ~45 min from Millburn. (~$25)

SUNDAY — Nyack, NY (~45 min out) — approx. $145 for the 3 of you
- 10:30am — City stroll: Nyack Main Street & Memorial Park, Nyack, NY. Colorful storefronts and local art on Main St, then a walk along the Hudson at Memorial Park for river views. ~45 min from Millburn. (free)
- 12:00pm — Culture: Edward Hopper House Museum & Study Center, Nyack, NY. The artist's childhood home, a manageable size for a quick family visit, with a short film and his childhood drawings on display. ~45 min from Millburn. (~$25)
- 1:00pm — Lunch: Hudson House, Nyack, NY — New American right on the river, fresh ingredients, laid-back atmosphere. ~45 min from Millburn. (~$90)
- 2:30pm — Dessert: Temptations Café, Nyack, NY — sundaes and pastries with sidewalk seating on Main St for people-watching. ~45 min from Millburn. (~$20)

IF IT RAINS
- Saturday swap: Chester Antique Center, Chester, NJ — a large indoor multi-dealer antique mall on Rt 206, easy browsing if the waterfall hike is out. Keep lunch and dessert as planned.
- Sunday swap: Lean into the Edward Hopper House Museum as the main event and skip the outdoor Main St/Memorial Park walk — lunch and dessert are both indoor anyway, so the rest of the day holds.
