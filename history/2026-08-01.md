SATURDAY — Hudson Valley day (~75 min out)
- 10:00am — Hike: Bear Mountain State Park, Hessian Lake Loop, Bear Mountain, NY. Easy, mostly-paved 1.4-mile loop around the lake at the foot of the mountain; drive up to Perkins Memorial Tower after if legs hold out. ~75 min from Millburn.
- 12:15pm — Lunch: Pepe's Tacos, Highland Falls, NY — casual Mexican, tacos and quesadillas, a few minutes from the park. ~80 min from Millburn.
- 1:30pm — Culture: Storm King Art Center, New Windsor, NY. 500 walkable acres of large-scale outdoor sculpture — the kind of huge, strange pieces a 10-year-old actually likes. ~90 min from Millburn — a little past our usual radius, but it's 15 minutes from Bear Mountain, so one drive covers both.
- 4:00pm — Dessert: Moo Moo's Creamery, Cornwall-on-Hudson, NY — homemade ice cream on the village circle. ~85 min from Millburn.

SUNDAY — Astoria, Queens (~55 min out)
- 11:00am — City stroll: Astoria Park, Astoria, Queens, NY. Waterfront esplanade under the Hell Gate and RFK bridges with big river views; walk a few blocks over to Socrates Sculpture Park, a free East River sculpture park with Manhattan skyline views, for a low-key add-on. ~55 min from Millburn.
- 1:00pm — Lunch: Taverna Kyclades, Astoria, Queens, NY — Greek seafood, the neighborhood's most-loved taverna (expect a short wait, no reservations). ~55 min from Millburn.
- 2:30pm — Dessert: Omonia Cafe, Astoria, Queens, NY — Greek bakery/patisserie, known for baklava cheesecake. ~55 min from Millburn.

IF IT RAINS
- Saturday swap: West Point Museum, Highland Falls, NY — indoor military history museum near Bear Mountain (real exhibits — weapons, uniforms, war history — not a kids' museum). Keep lunch and dessert as planned.
- Sunday swap: Museum of the Moving Image, Astoria, Queens, NY — indoor, interactive, right in the same neighborhood so no extra driving. Keep lunch and dessert as planned.
