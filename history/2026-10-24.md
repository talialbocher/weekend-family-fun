Subject: Weekend Itinerary — October 24 to October 25

Weather: Saturday looks mostly sunny and cool, highs ~60-63°F, breezy — good hiking weather. Sunday is similar, partly sunny, mid-upper 50s to low 60s, staying dry through the day with rain chances only arriving late Sunday night.

=== SATURDAY — Long Island Gold Coast ===

1. Caumsett State Historic Park Preserve — Fall Seasonal Stroll, 9:45-11:45am
   Ranger-led 1.5-mile fall color walk through the old Marshall Field estate; $4/person, reservation required — call (631) 423-1770 ahead. If you can't get a spot, the park's paved 3-mile loop past the historic mansion is a great self-guided walk anyway.
   Lloyd Harbor/Huntington, NY — ~1h45m drive (allow 2h with weekend traffic)

2. Cold Spring Harbor — Main Street & harborfront stroll, ~12:15pm
   Small whaling-era village street right on the water — easy, scenic walk before lunch.
   Cold Spring Harbor, NY (Suffolk County — not the Cold Spring, NY we did a few weeks ago) — ~10 min from Caumsett

3. Lunch: Sandbar, ~12:45pm
   Casual American comfort food and seafood, village spot overlooking the harbor.
   Cold Spring Harbor, NY — same stop as above

4. Dessert: Cream Bakery & Cafe, ~2:30pm
   Known for mini cakes, custom cakes, and donut cakes — walkable Main Street spot.
   260 Main St, Huntington, NY — ~10 min from Cold Spring Harbor

Rough cost: ~$180 for the day (the Long Island tolls/distance push it over budget — worth it for a fresh fall-foliage program and a new part of the map).

=== SUNDAY — Hunterdon County / Delaware River valley ===

5. Melick's Town Farm — apple picking, ~11:00am
   Pick-your-own apples (still going through early November) plus a farm market and cider.
   19 King St, Oldwick, NJ — ~40 min drive

6. Frenchtown — Bridge Street historic district stroll, ~12:30pm
   Little river town on the Delaware — galleries, antique shops, and a towpath right along the water.
   Frenchtown, NJ — ~40 min from Oldwick

7. Lunch: Frenchtown Inn, ~1:00pm
   American bistro menu, indoor/outdoor seating overlooking the river.
   7 Bridge St, Frenchtown, NJ — same stop as above

8. Dessert: Libbybeans, ~2:30pm
   Café and candy shop known for gelato and chocolate, with a quiet backyard seating area.
   Frenchtown, NJ — same stop as above

Rough cost: ~$145 for the day — on budget.

=== IF IT RAINS ===

Saturday swap: Heckscher Museum of Art (indoor), Prime Ave, Huntington, NY, in place of Caumsett/Cold Spring Harbor outdoor time. Note the Fall Seasonal Stroll itself would likely be cancelled in real rain anyway, so call ahead if skies look iffy.

Sunday swap: Prallsville Mills, Stockton, NJ — a historic 19th-century grist mill complex with gallery space, a few minutes from Frenchtown — swap in for the outdoor Bridge Street browsing if it's wet.

New this week: Caumsett State Historic Park Preserve, Cold Spring Harbor village & Sandbar, Cream Bakery & Cafe (Huntington), Melick's Town Farm (Oldwick), Frenchtown (Bridge Street, Frenchtown Inn, Libbybeans), plus rain alts Heckscher Museum of Art and Prallsville Mills — nothing repeated from the ledger.
