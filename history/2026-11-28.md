Subject: Weekend Itinerary — November 28 to November 29

WEATHER
Saturday: Cold but mostly dry with decent sun, highs near upper 40s — good hiking weather.
Sunday: Clouds build through the day with a chance of scattered showers, especially by afternoon, highs mid-40s — the lower-key day below is built with that in mind.

SATURDAY — Western NJ (Warren County)

10:00am — Hike: Jenny Jump State Forest, Ghost Lake Trail, Hope, NJ
Easy-moderate 1.8-mile blue-blazed loop past Ghost Lake and old iron-mine ruins, through quiet fall woods with a summit overlook. ~55 min from Millburn.

12:30pm — Lunch: James on Main, Hackettstown, NJ
New American in a rustic brick-building space on Main St — a real menu, not a kids' one. ~15 min from Jenny Jump.

1:45pm — Dessert: Main Street Ice Cream & Creperie, Hackettstown, NJ
Ice cream and crepes, a couple doors down from lunch. Same stop.

Approx. cost: ~$100 (parking, lunch, dessert, gas).

SUNDAY — Rye, NY (Westchester)

10:30am — Nature walk: Edith G. Read Wildlife Sanctuary, Rye, NY
179-acre Long Island Sound shoreline sanctuary — flat trails, a half-mile of beach, wintering ducks and osprey. Low-key, under an hour of walking. ~55 min from Millburn.

12:30pm — Lunch: Rafele Rye, Rye, NY
Italian bistro on Purchase St, Michelin Bib Gourmand — good pasta, easygoing with a kid at the table. ~5 min from the sanctuary.

1:45pm — City stroll: Purchase Street & the Rye waterfront esplanade, Rye, NY
Walkable downtown strip of shops, then down toward the water. Same area as lunch.

2:30pm — Dessert: Longford's Ice Cream, Rye, NY
Small-batch ice cream and gelato, Rye institution since 1992.

3:00pm — Culture (optional add-on): Rye Arts Center, Rye, NY
Rotating gallery exhibits, free to walk through if you want one more stop before heading home.

Approx. cost: ~$140 (lunch runs a bit more here, which is the one thing pushing this day toward the top of budget — worth it for the Bib Gourmand pasta).

IF IT RAINS
Saturday: Swap Jenny Jump for the Pequest Trout Hatchery & Natural Resource Education Center, Oxford, NJ — indoor nature center with large aquariums and wildlife exhibits, ~15 min from Hackettstown. Keep lunch and dessert as planned (both indoors already).
Sunday: Swap the Read Sanctuary walk for the Neuberger Museum of Art, Purchase, NY — a real art museum, ~10 min from Rye. Keep lunch, the stroll (just shorten it), and dessert as planned.

New this week: Jenny Jump State Forest, Hackettstown's James on Main and Main Street Ice Cream & Creperie, and Rye, NY (Edith G. Read Wildlife Sanctuary, Rafele Rye, Purchase Street, Longford's Ice Cream, Rye Arts Center) — nothing repeated from the last several weeks.
