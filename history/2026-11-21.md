Subject: Weekend Itinerary — November 21 to November 22

WEATHER
Saturday: Mild and partly sunny, high near 58-60°F — good hiking weather.
Sunday: Cooler and mostly cloudy, high upper 40s to low 50s, slight chance of light showers.

SATURDAY, NOV 21 — Long Island North Shore

9:00 AM — Depart Millburn (drive ~85 min)
10:30 AM — Hike: Sunken Meadow State Park, Kings Park, NY. Bluff Trail along the Nissequogue River with wide views over Smithtown Bay; moderate, well-graded climb, roughly 2-3 miles — easy for the whole family. Drive: ~85 min.
12:30 PM — Lunch: Main Street Cafe, Northport, NY — casual seafood & American right on the harbor. Drive: ~10 min from the park.
1:30 PM — City stroll: Northport Village Main Street & Harborfront, Northport, NY — shops along Main St and a walk out onto the harbor docks.
2:30 PM — Dessert: Northport Sweet Shop, Northport, NY — a 96-year-old luncheonette famous for old-fashioned ice cream sodas, shakes, and malts.

~$175 today — the extra is the Long Island Sound drive; genuinely new territory after months of repeat Hudson Valley/NJ hikes.

SUNDAY, NOV 22 — close to home

10:00 AM — Low-key walk: Cora Hartshorn Arboretum & Bird Sanctuary, Short Hills, NJ — quiet, easy wooded trails minutes from home, about 3 miles total. Drive: ~10 min.
11:30 AM — City stroll: Downtown Cranford & the Rahway River Parkway, Cranford, NJ — a walkable, under-the-radar river town. Drive: ~20 min.
12:15 PM — Lunch: Garlic Rose Bistro, Cranford, NJ — garlic-forward contemporary Italian-American, across from the train station.
1:30 PM — Dessert: Vanilla Bean Creamery, Cranford, NJ — small-batch ice cream made in-house, vegan options too.

~$125 today.

Weekend total: ~$300 (~$150/day average) — Saturday runs a bit over for the Long Island drive, Sunday comes in under to balance it.

IF IT RAINS
- Saturday: swap the Sunken Meadow hike for the Vanderbilt Museum & Planetarium, Centerport, NY — indoor 1930s mansion, marine museum, and a planetarium show, 10 minutes from Northport.
- Sunday: swap the Cora Hartshorn walk for the Macculloch Hall Historical Museum & Gardens, Morristown, NJ — indoor historic house tour, ~20 min from Millburn.
- Lunch and dessert both days are already indoors, so no change needed there.

New this week: Sunken Meadow State Park, Northport (Main Street Cafe, Northport Village, Northport Sweet Shop), Cora Hartshorn Arboretum, downtown Cranford (Garlic Rose Bistro, Vanilla Bean Creamery) — plus rain-backup picks Vanderbilt Museum & Planetarium and Macculloch Hall Historical Museum. Nothing repeated from the last several weeks.
