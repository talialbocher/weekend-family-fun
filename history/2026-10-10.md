Subject: Weekend Itinerary — October 10 to October 11

Weather: Saturday is looking mild and mostly sunny, high near 71°F/low 50°F, very low rain chance. Sunday is a bit cooler and cloudier, high near 68°F/low 52°F, still mostly dry.

SATURDAY — Ringwood, NJ (hike + garden day)

9:30am — Shepherd Lake Loop Trail, Ringwood State Park, Ringwood, NJ (~50 min from Millburn)
Easy, flat ~2-mile loop around a scenic lake with fall color reflecting off the water. Low-key and kid-paced.

11:15am — New Jersey Botanical Garden at Skylands Manor, Ringwood, NJ (same park, no drive)
Paved paths through formal gardens and woodland at the old Skylands estate, with the Ramapo Mountains as a backdrop. A nice change of pace after the lake loop.

1:00pm — Tony's Pizza, Pompton Lakes, NJ (~15 min from Ringwood)
Family-owned, casual pizzeria with a cozy vibe — straightforward lunch, no fuss.

2:30pm — Randy's Homemade Ice Cream, Pompton Lakes, NJ (same town)
Locally loved ice cream shop (4.8 stars, 128+ reviews) — good way to close out the day.

Rough cost: ~$90-100 (park admission if charged this time of year, lunch, ice cream, gas).

SUNDAY — Red Hook, Brooklyn (waterfront stroll day)

10:30am — Valentino Pier (Louis Valentino Jr. Park & Pier), Red Hook, Brooklyn, NY (~55 min from Millburn)
A quiet waterfront pier with a head-on view of the Statue of Liberty and the Manhattan skyline — none of the crowds of the ferry line.

12:30pm — Red Hook Lobster Pound, Red Hook, Brooklyn, NY (short walk)
Maine lobster rolls and casual seafood — a Red Hook institution since 2009.

1:45pm — Steve's Authentic Key Lime Pie, Red Hook, Brooklyn, NY (short walk)
Known for real key-lime-juice pie with a graham cracker crust, plus chocolate-dipped "swingles" on a stick. Look for the vintage pale Ford panel truck out front.

2:30pm — Pioneer Works, Red Hook, Brooklyn, NY (short walk)
Arts center with rotating exhibitions; October 11 looks to land on one of their free "Second Sundays" open-house days (live music, hands-on activities, all ages) — worth confirming the day-of schedule on their site, but the galleries are open and free either way.

Rough cost: ~$140-160, a bit over our usual target — the extra is NYC tolls/parking plus lobster rolls, and Red Hook is a genuinely new spot worth the trip.

IF IT RAINS

Saturday: Skip the lake loop and do an indoor guided tour of the Ringwood Manor House instead (same park, covered) — then keep lunch and ice cream as planned.
Sunday: Skip the pier walk and spend more time in Pioneer Works' indoor galleries; Red Hook Lobster Pound and Steve's both have indoor seating too.

New this week: Ringwood State Park / Shepherd Lake, NJ Botanical Garden at Skylands Manor, Pompton Lakes (Tony's Pizza + Randy's Homemade Ice Cream), and Red Hook, Brooklyn (Valentino Pier, Red Hook Lobster Pound, Steve's Key Lime Pie, Pioneer Works) — nothing repeated from the last several weekends.
