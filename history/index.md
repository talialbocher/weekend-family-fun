before planning and appends one section to the top after each run.

that stops the itinerary from recommending Carlo's Bakery for the fourth time.

Format: one `##` section per weekend, with every *named* place listed. Generic
suggestions ("a Korean spot in Fort Lee") get listed too, tagged `[vague]`, so
the pattern is visible even when the pick wasn't specific.
