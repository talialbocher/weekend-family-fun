Subject: Weekend Itinerary — October 3 to October 4

Weather: Saturday — mostly sunny and warm, high near 72°F, clear evening. Sunday — clouds increasing through the day, showers developing later in the afternoon/evening, high near 67°F (~50% chance of rain).

SATURDAY — Upper Manhattan (Inwood / Washington Heights, ~40-45 min from Millburn)

- 10:00am — Hike/nature: Inwood Hill Park, Manhattan — the Orange or Blue Trail loop (~1.7 mi) through Manhattan's only natural forest, with old-growth trees, a salt marsh, and Hudson/Palisades views. ~45 min drive.
- 12:30pm — Lunch: Pick and Eat, Washington Heights, Manhattan — eclectic, kid-friendly American (burgers, pizza, tacos, wraps) made from local/organic ingredients. ~10 min from the park.
- 1:30pm — City stroll + event: Little Red Lighthouse Festival, Fort Washington Park & the Hudson River Greenway under the GW Bridge, Washington Heights — live music and family activities celebrating Manhattan's only lighthouse, 12-4pm. Same area.
- 3:30pm — Dessert: Carrot Top Pastries, Washington Heights — famous for carrot cake and carrot muffins. ~5 min away.

Approx cost: ~$110 (lunch for 3, dessert, parking/gas) — under budget.

SUNDAY — Bergen/Passaic County, NJ (~35-40 min from Millburn)

- 9:30am — Other/seasonal: Demarest Farms, Hillsdale, NJ — pumpkin and apple picking, hayride, petting zoo, and fresh apple cider donuts (the morning's "dessert"). ~40 min drive. Doing this before the afternoon clouds roll in.
- 12:30pm — Lunch: Christopher's a Neighborhood Place, Wayne, NJ — New American, organic/locally-sourced, casual and family-friendly. ~25 min from Hillsdale.
- 1:30pm — Culture (indoor, built for the rain): Dey Mansion, Wayne, NJ — George Washington's 1780 headquarters, a Revolutionary War house-museum with tours on the hour. Open 9am-5pm. Same area as lunch — a good indoor anchor as the rain moves in.

Approx cost: ~$140 (picking fees, cider donuts, lunch for 3, museum, gas) — right at budget, worth it for the full fall-farm day before the rain.

IF IT RAINS

- Saturday swap (if rain arrives despite the forecast): The Cloisters, Fort Tryon Park, Manhattan — the Met's medieval art branch, an indoor stand-in for the Inwood hike and lighthouse festival, same neighborhood, same drive time.
- Sunday swap (if rain starts earlier than expected): Skip the outdoor picking at Demarest Farms — go straight to Dey Mansion (opens 9am), then Paterson Great Falls National Historical Park visitor center, Paterson, NJ, for indoor exhibits on the falls and the city's industrial history. ~15 min from Wayne.

New this week: Inwood Hill Park, the Little Red Lighthouse Festival/Fort Washington Park, Pick and Eat, Carrot Top Pastries, Demarest Farms, Christopher's a Neighborhood Place, Dey Mansion, plus rain-backup picks The Cloisters and Paterson Great Falls — nothing repeated from the last several weeks, and neither day reuses a town from the last two weekends (Warwick/Tuxedo, Yonkers, Riverdale, or Stanhope/Sparta/Glen Cove/Sea Cliff).
