Weather: Saturday — mostly cloudy, high 66°F, rain moves in mainly after 2pm (40% during the day, 70% overnight). Sunday — rain likely all day with gusty NE wind to 37mph, high 64°F, 3/4"-1" of rain expected. Outdoor stuff is front-loaded into Saturday morning; Sunday is built indoors on purpose.

SATURDAY — Tarrytown / Sleepy Hollow, NY (~50 min from Millburn)

9:00am — Hike: Rockefeller State Park Preserve, Pocantico Hills/Sleepy Hollow, NY. Easy-moderate carriage-road loop around Swan Lake through woods and open meadow — well suited to a 10-year-old, and an early start beats the afternoon rain. $6 parking. ~50 min drive.

11:00am — City stroll: Tarrytown Main Street & the RiverWalk waterfront promenade on the Hudson. Same area as the hike, a few minutes away. Shops and river views before the weather turns.

12:30pm — Lunch: RiverMarket Bar and Kitchen, Tarrytown, NY — New American, farm-to-fork with Hudson Valley ingredients.

2:00pm — Culture (timed for the rain): Lyndhurst Mansion, Tarrytown, NY — one-hour guided tour of the Gothic Revival mansion, ~16 rooms of Gilded Age decorative arts. Good indoor cover once the rain arrives.

3:30pm — Dessert: Noble Pies, Tarrytown, NY — family-run bakery known for both sweet and savory pies.

~$160 today — a bit over budget; the Lyndhurst tour is the reason, and it's real Gilded Age history rather than a gift-shop stop.

SUNDAY — Ironbound, Newark, NJ (~20-25 min from Millburn) — built around the all-day rain

10:00am — Culture: Newark Museum of Art, Newark, NJ. Real collection (American art, Tibetan galleries, planetarium) — a solid full morning indoors while the wind and rain are worst.

12:00pm — Lunch: Seabra's Marisqueira, Ironbound, Newark, NJ — Portuguese seafood spot open since 1989, known for octopus.

1:30pm — Stroll: Ferry Street, Ironbound, Newark, NJ — the neighborhood's main shopping/dining strip, awnings overhead for browsing between raindrops.

2:00pm — Dessert: Teixeira's Bakery, Ironbound, Newark, NJ — Portuguese pastéis de nata and fresh-baked bread.

~$125 today — under budget.
