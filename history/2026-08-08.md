Weather: Saturday looks warm and humid (upper 80s/low 90s) with a chance of a scattered afternoon thunderstorm. Sunday is drier and a touch more comfortable — partly cloudy, high near 89°F.

SATURDAY, AUGUST 8

9:30 AM — Hike: Sourland Mountain Preserve, Hillsborough, NJ
Moderate 2.2-mile loop through a boulder-strewn glacial rock forest, mostly shaded — good pick for a warm morning before any afternoon storms roll in. ~45 min from Millburn.

12:00 PM — Lunch: Nomad Pizza, Hopewell, NJ — wood-fired Neapolitan pizza
Casual, laid-back dining room, easy with a kid. ~50 min from Millburn.

1:15 PM — Dessert: Uncle Ed's Creamery, Hopewell, NJ — small-batch ice cream
Same town as lunch, no extra driving.

2:30 PM — Low-key extra: Lambertville historic downtown & canal towpath, NJ
Antique shops, galleries, and an easy stroll along the Delaware — mostly indoor browsing, so it's a natural place to be if a storm passes through. ~65 min from Millburn.

SUNDAY, AUGUST 9

10:00 AM — City stroll + Event: Flushing Meadows Corona Park, Queens, NY
Walk the promenade around the Unisphere and Meadow Lake. The Hong Kong Dragon Boat Festival is on both days this weekend — free live music, an international food court, and boat races on the lake. ~55 min from Millburn.

11:30 AM — Culture: Queens Museum, Flushing Meadows Corona Park, Queens, NY
See the famous Panorama of the City of New York — a 9,335-sq-ft scale model of all five boroughs. Right in the same park.

1:00 PM — Lunch: Nan Xiang Soup Dumplings, Flushing, NY — Shanghainese soup dumplings
A Flushing institution; go a little before or after the noon rush. ~10 min from the park.

2:15 PM — Dessert: Spot Dessert Bar, Flushing, NY — creative desserts & bubble tea
Honey toast with condensed-milk ice cream is the thing to get.

IF IT RAINS

Saturday: swap the Sourland hike for Grounds For Sculpture in Hamilton, NJ — indoor galleries plus covered pavilions, ~50 min from Millburn. Keep lunch and dessert in Hopewell (about 20 min away).

Sunday: skip the outdoor park walk and spend more time inside the Queens Museum, then eat at the New World Mall food court in Flushing instead of moving between separate spots — everything indoors and a few minutes apart.

Approx cost: Saturday ~$100 (free parking/trail, pizza lunch, ice cream, gas). Sunday ~$145 (Queens parking, museum admission, dumpling lunch, dessert bar, gas) — both within the usual budget.

New this week: Sourland Mountain Preserve, Hopewell NJ (Nomad Pizza, Uncle Ed's Creamery), Lambertville, Flushing Meadows Corona Park & the Dragon Boat Festival, Queens Museum, Nan Xiang Soup Dumplings, Spot Dessert Bar — nothing repeated from the last 6 weeks.
