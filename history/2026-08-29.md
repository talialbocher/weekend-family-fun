# Weekend Itinerary — 08-29 to 08-30

Subject: Weekend Itinerary — 08-29 to 08-30

WEATHER
Saturday: Warm, ~80°F, chance of afternoon/evening thunderstorms.
Sunday: Mostly sunny and clear, low-to-mid 80s.

Since Saturday carries the storm risk, that day stays close to home with easy indoor bailouts. Sunday's the clear day, so that's when we go longer for the hike.

=== SATURDAY — Red Bank, NJ (~50 min from Millburn) ===

10:00 AM — City stroll: Broad Street & Monmouth Street, downtown Red Bank
Boutique shops and a walkable historic downtown along the Navesink River. Easy to duck indoors if it clouds up.
Drive: ~50 min

12:00 PM — Lunch: Elli's Backyard, Red Bank, NJ — American/Italian
Family-owned since 2021, casual, kid-easy menu (also gluten-free/vegan options).
Drive: same area

1:30 PM — Dessert: Fable Creamery, Red Bank, NJ
Plant-based ice cream shop with inventive flavors and fresh cookies — a genuinely different dessert stop.
Drive: same area

5:00–7:00 PM — Event: The Great Whaleboat Race, Lake Lefferts Park, Matawan, NJ
Free community event for America's 250th — first-responder crews from Matawan, Aberdeen, and Keyport row across the lake to fetch their mayors, plus colonial reenactors, live music, food, and kids' games/inflatables. Fun, low-key, not a wait-in-line kind of thing.
Drive: ~50 min from Millburn, ~15 min from Red Bank

Rough Saturday cost: ~$110 (lunch + dessert + gas; the evening event is free)

=== SUNDAY — Beacon & Poughkeepsie, NY (~90 min from Millburn) ===

The longer drive is worth it: a real fire-tower hike, a mile-long walk over the Hudson, and Beacon's newer food scene, all in one trip.

9:30 AM — Hike: Mount Beacon Park loop trail, Beacon, NY
~1 mile up to the old fire-tower foundation, moderate grade, big payoff — Hudson Highlands views and a glimpse of the river. Free parking at Rt 9D & Howland Ave.
Drive: ~90 min

11:00 AM — Lunch: Oui Oui Cuisine, Beacon, NY — French bistro
New brick-and-mortar spot on Eliza St: croque sandwiches, French onion soup, steak frites.
Drive: same area

12:15 PM — Dessert: Beacon Bread Company, Beacon, NY
Newly opened bakery — breads and pastries, good for a to-go treat.
Drive: same area

1:30 PM — City stroll: Walkway Over the Hudson, Poughkeepsie/Highland, NY
A flat, paved 1.28-mile former rail bridge, 212 feet above the river — sweeping views, no climbing.
Drive: ~20 min from Beacon

3:00 PM — Culture: Frances Lehman Loeb Art Center, Vassar College, Poughkeepsie, NY
Free art museum on Vassar's campus — antiquity to contemporary, a relaxed indoor wind-down before the drive home.
Drive: same area

Rough Sunday cost: ~$120 (lunch + dessert + gas/tolls; hike, walkway, and museum are all free)

=== IF IT RAINS ===

Saturday swap: Skip the outdoor stroll and evening event. Basie Center Cinemas, Red Bank (indie films, four screens) covers the afternoon; Broad Street's shops are indoors anyway for browsing.

Sunday swap: Skip the hike and Walkway. Spend the day at the Frances Lehman Loeb Art Center (already free/indoor) and browse Beacon's indoor spots — Hudson Beach Glass Gallery (glassblowing studio/gallery, 162 Main St) is a good one — instead of the river walk.
