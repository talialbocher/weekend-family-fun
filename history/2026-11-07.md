Subject: Weekend Itinerary — November 7 to November 8

Weather: Saturday looks unsettled — chance of showers in the morning, clearing to partly cloudy by afternoon, high near 58°F. Sunday looks milder and drier — mix of sun and clouds, high near 60°F. (Forecasts this far out are still soft — worth a last check Friday night.)

Because Saturday's the dicier day, we kept it to a short, easy-to-shift drive; Sunday (the better day) gets the longer outing.

SATURDAY — Long Valley / Chester / Hackettstown, NJ (~45 min from Millburn)

- ~10:00 AM — Schooley's Mountain Park, Long Valley, NJ (~45 min). Boulder Gorge waterfall loop, ~2.3 miles, easy grade. If the morning showers are still going, push start time back an hour or swap to the rain plan below.
- ~12:00 PM — Alstede Farms, Chester, NJ (~50 min). Pick-your-own season just wrapped, but the farm market, bakery, and cider mill are still running — good for a quick browse and cider donuts.
- ~12:30 PM — Lunch: Valley Restaurant, Long Valley, NJ (~45 min) — family-run American diner, been there since 1978.
- ~2:30 PM — Dessert: Dalcourt's Desserts, Hackettstown, NJ (~55 min) — gourmet dessert shop, worth the short detour.

Rough cost: ~$110 for the day (lunch, farm market treats, dessert, gas) — under budget.

SUNDAY — Piermont / Palisades, NY (~50 min from Millburn)

- ~10:00 AM — Tallman Mountain State Park, Palisades/Piermont, NY (~50 min). Hudson River Overlook Loop, ~3.7 miles, easy, with views over the river and Piermont Marsh.
- ~12:00 PM — City stroll: Piermont Avenue & waterfront, Piermont, NY (~50 min) — small riverfront village, shops and galleries along the main drag.
- ~12:15 PM — Lunch: O Lar, Piermont, NY (~50 min) — casual pizza and Greek-inspired salads/seafood.
- ~2:00 PM — Dessert: Piermont Creamery, Piermont, NY (~50 min) — local ice cream.

Rough cost: ~$100 for the day — under budget.

IF IT RAINS

- Saturday swap: Liberty Village Premium Outlets, Flemington, NJ (~50 min) — indoor browsing, hot drinks, skip the hike.
- Sunday swap: Palisades Center Mall, West Nyack, NY (~50 min) — indoor mall with an aquarium, ice rink, and movie theater, about 15 min from Piermont.

New this week: Schooley's Mountain Park, Alstede Farms, Valley Restaurant, and Dalcourt's Desserts (Long Valley/Chester/Hackettstown, NJ); Tallman Mountain State Park, Piermont Avenue, O Lar, and Piermont Creamery (Piermont, NY); Liberty Village Premium Outlets and Palisades Center Mall as rain backups — nothing repeated from recent weeks.
