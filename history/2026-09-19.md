Subject: Weekend Itinerary — September 19 to September 20

WEATHER
Saturday: Mostly sunny and warm, high near 79°F, low around 66°F — no rain signal, great hiking weather.
Sunday: Mostly sunny, high near 77°F, low around 66°F — another dry, comfortable day.

SATURDAY (Sep 19) — Sussex County, NJ (~55–60 min from Millburn, all in one area)

9:30 AM — Hike/nature: Sussex Branch Trail at Waterloo Village, Allamuchy Mountain State Park, Stanhope, NJ (~55 min)
Easy-moderate ~3-mile out-and-back along an old rail bed beside the Musconetcong River and old canal ponds, ending at the restored 19th-century Waterloo Village canal town — general store, gristmill, and blacksmith shop still standing to poke around. Flat, no scrambling.

12:00 PM — Lunch: Krogh's Restaurant & Brew Pub, Sparta, NJ — American brewpub (~60 min)
A Sussex County institution — burgers, wings, flatbreads, big portions, easy with a kid at the table.

1:30 PM — Dessert: Sparta Dairy, Sparta, NJ — ice cream (~60 min)
Old-school ice cream stand with creekside seating near Engerman Field — soft serve, sundaes, hard ice cream.

Rough cost: ~$115 (trail parking, brewpub lunch, ice cream, gas).

SUNDAY (Sep 20) — Sea Cliff / Glen Cove, NY (~80 min from Millburn — Long Island's North Shore, genuinely new territory, worth the longer drive and bridge tolls)

9:30 AM — Hike/nature: Garvies Point Preserve Loop, Glen Cove, NY (~80 min)
Easy 1.8-mile loop through bluff-top woods down to a Long Island Sound beach — glacial geology, good birdwatching, no steep sections.

10:45 AM — Culture: Garvies Point Museum, Glen Cove, NY (same stop, no extra drive)
Small, kid-good museum on the preserve grounds — local geology and Native American archaeology exhibits. 20–30 minutes.

11:30 AM — City stroll: Sea Cliff Village, Sea Cliff, NY (~85 min)
A tiny, hilly Victorian village on the Sound — Sea Cliff Avenue's indie shops and galleries, the Cliff Walk, and Gerard Street Park for a waterfront overlook of the water and Manhattan skyline on a clear day.

1:00 PM — Lunch: La Spezia Ristorante, Sea Cliff, NY — family-style Southern Italian (~85 min)
Hearty shared platters — carbonara, calamari, ravioli — passed around the table like a big Sunday dinner.

2:30 PM — Dessert: Dessertino & The Crafty Spoon, Sea Cliff, NY — coffee, gelato & desserts (~85 min)
Right on Sea Cliff Avenue, easy walk from the stroll — gelato, ice cream, espresso.

Rough cost: ~$150 (preserve/museum admission, Italian lunch for three, dessert, gas + bridge tolls). The tolls are the overage — Long Island's Gold Coast is new ground for us and worth the trip.

IF IT RAINS

Saturday: Swap the Sussex Branch Trail hike for the indoor Sussex County Historical Society Museum, Newton, NJ (~65 min) — local history in a 19th-century building. Keep lunch at Krogh's (full indoor seating). Swap the ice cream stand for Alpine Creamery, Sparta, NJ — same idea, indoor seating instead of a walk-up window.

Sunday: Swap the Garvies Point hike and the outdoor Sea Cliff stroll for the Nassau County Museum of Art, Roslyn, NY (~75 min) — real collection, indoor galleries, plus a conservatory. Keep lunch at La Spezia and dessert at Dessertino (both have indoor seating already).

New this week: Sussex Branch Trail & Waterloo Village, Krogh's Restaurant & Brew Pub, Sparta Dairy (Sparta, NJ); Garvies Point Preserve & Museum, Sea Cliff Village, La Spezia Ristorante, and Dessertino & The Crafty Spoon (Sea Cliff/Glen Cove, NY) — nothing repeated from the last several weeks, and no town reused from the last two weekends (Greenwich CT, Blairstown/Delaware Water Gap NJ, Boonton NJ, New Brunswick/Highland Park NJ).
