Subject: Weekend Itinerary — October 31 to November 1

WEATHER
Saturday (Halloween): Mild and cloudy, chance of light showers in the morning clearing by afternoon, high near 60°F.
Sunday: Partly cloudy and a touch cooler, high in the mid-50s, lower rain chance.

SATURDAY, OCT 31 — Westchester, NY (~50-65 min drive from Millburn)

10:00am — Teatown Lake Reservation, Ossining, NY (~60 min drive)
Hike/nature. Lakeside Loop + Hidden Valley circuit, ~3 easy miles around the lake with a boardwalk and bird blind. $7 parking.

12:30pm — Lunch: The Boathouse, Ossining, NY (~5 min from Teatown)
Casual waterside American/seafood spot right on the Hudson — order at the counter, eat on the deck.

2:00pm — Croton Point Park waterfront walk, Croton-on-Hudson, NY (~10 min from Ossining)
City stroll / nature. A flat, scenic loop out onto the Hudson River peninsula — big open river views, easy for everyone.

3:30pm — Dessert: The Blue Pig, Croton-on-Hudson, NY
Homemade ice cream (plus pies and sandwiches if anyone wants a second round).

6:30pm — Event (optional, book ahead): The Great Jack O'Lantern Blaze, Van Cortlandt Manor, Croton-on-Hudson, NY
Thousands of hand-carved, illuminated pumpkins along a lantern-lit trail — the quintessential Halloween-night outing, and it's actually Halloween. Needs timed tickets bought in advance (hudsonvalley.org) and these sell out — check availability now. ~$24-36/person plus parking.

Approx. Saturday cost: ~$210 for the three of you (parking + lunch + ice cream + Blaze tickets). The overage is the Blaze — it's a once-a-year, kid-loves-pumpkins thing and it's actually Halloween night.

SUNDAY, NOV 1 — City Island & Pelham Bay, Bronx, NY (~55-65 min drive from Millburn)

10:00am — Kazimiroff Nature Trail, Hunter Island, Pelham Bay Park, Bronx, NY (~60 min drive)
Easy nature walk (bonus, not the main hike). Blue Loop, ~1.25 miles around Hunter Island — rocky shoreline, lagoons, big NYC-nature-you-didn't-expect feel.

12:00pm — City stroll: City Island Avenue, City Island, Bronx, NY (~5 min from Pelham Bay)
A real seaside-village main street — marinas, antique shops, lobster boats — that happens to be inside city limits.

12:30pm — Lunch: Johnny's Reef Restaurant, City Island, Bronx, NY
Order-at-the-counter fried and fresh seafood, eaten at picnic tables looking out over Long Island Sound.

2:00pm — Culture (low-key): City Island Nautical Museum, City Island, Bronx, NY
Small, old-schoolhouse museum on the island's maritime and boatbuilding history. Quick, no-line stop.

2:45pm — Dessert: Lickety Split, City Island, Bronx, NY
20+ flavors of ice cream and gelato out of a tiny wooden cottage, with an outdoor patio.

Approx. Sunday cost: ~$105 for the three of you — comfortably under budget.

IF IT RAINS
Saturday swap: Katonah Museum of Art, Katonah, NY (~45 min drive) in place of Teatown and Croton Point — small, well-regarded contemporary art museum, easy for a 10-year-old. Keep lunch at The Boathouse (indoor seating) and dessert at The Blue Pig. The Blaze runs rain or shine unless it's severe weather — check hudsonvalley.org day-of.
Sunday swap: Bartow-Pell Mansion Museum, Pelham Bay Park, Bronx, NY — an 1842 mansion and formal gardens, a few minutes from City Island, in place of the Kazimiroff Trail. City Island lunch, museum, and dessert all stay as planned (all indoor/covered).

New this week: Teatown Lake Reservation, The Boathouse, Croton Point Park, The Blue Pig, and the Great Jack O'Lantern Blaze (all Ossining/Croton-on-Hudson, Westchester); Kazimiroff Nature Trail, City Island Avenue, Johnny's Reef, City Island Nautical Museum, and Lickety Split (City Island/Pelham Bay, Bronx) — nothing repeated from the last several weekends, and a brand-new region (Westchester) we haven't used before.
