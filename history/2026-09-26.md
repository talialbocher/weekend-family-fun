Subject: Weekend Itinerary — September 26 to September 27

Weather: Saturday — warm and increasingly cloudy, high near 86°F, slight chance of evening thunderstorms after dark (day plans are fine). Sunday — sunny and pleasant, high near 83°F.

SATURDAY — Warwick Valley, NY (~55-65 min from Millburn)

- 10:00am — Hike: Sterling Lake Loop Trail, Sterling Forest State Park (Tuxedo/Warwick, NY). Easy, mostly flat 4.1-mile loop around the park's largest lake, passing old iron-mine ruins; the fire tower spur is an easy detour if he wants a climb. ~60 min drive.
- 12:30pm — Lunch: 100 Main, Warwick, NY — Mediterranean, on Warwick's Main Street. ~15 min from the trailhead.
- 1:30pm — City stroll: Warwick Village, Main St & South St — walkable historic downtown, shops and galleries. Same location as lunch.
- 3:00pm — Dessert: Bellvale Farms Creamery, Warwick, NY — homemade ice cream at the top of Mount Peter with panoramic farm views, famously one of the best ice cream stops in the country. ~10 min drive.

Approx cost: ~$110-120 (parking, lunch for 3, ice cream) — comfortably under budget.

SUNDAY — Yonkers / Riverdale, NY (~35-45 min from Millburn)

- 10:00am — City stroll / garden: Untermyer Park & Gardens, Yonkers, NY — free 43-acre Beaux-Arts garden with a walled garden, Temple of Love, and sweeping Hudson River views. A great easy morning walk.
- 11:30am — Culture/extra: Wave Hill, Riverdale, Bronx, NY — 28-acre public garden and cultural center overlooking the Hudson and Palisades; free weekend Family Art Project included with admission. ~10 min from Untermyer.
- 1:00pm — Lunch: Riverdale Diner, Riverdale, Bronx, NY — classic casual American diner.
- 2:30pm — Dessert: Lost Borough Ice Cream, Downtown Yonkers, NY — small-batch, handcrafted ice cream with playful local flavors.

Approx cost: ~$100-110 (garden admissions, lunch for 3, ice cream) — under budget.

IF IT RAINS

- Saturday swap: Sugar Loaf, NY — a small arts-and-crafts village with indoor galleries, pottery and jewelry studios to browse, about 15 min from Warwick. Keep lunch (100 Main) and dessert (Bellvale) as-is if the rain is intermittent.
- Sunday swap: Hudson River Museum & Planetarium, Yonkers, NY — art museum, historic mansion, and a digital planetarium, a 5-minute drive from Untermyer. Works well in place of the two outdoor gardens.

New this week: Sterling Forest State Park / Sterling Lake Loop, Warwick Village, 100 Main, Bellvale Farms Creamery, Untermyer Park & Gardens, Wave Hill, Riverdale Diner, Lost Borough Ice Cream, plus rain-backup picks Sugar Loaf NY and the Hudson River Museum — nothing repeated from the last several weeks, and neither day reuses a town from the last two weekends (Stanhope/Sparta/Glen Cove/Sea Cliff, or Greenwich/Delaware Water Gap/Blairstown).
