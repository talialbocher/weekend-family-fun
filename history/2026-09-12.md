SATURDAY (Sep 12) — Greenwich, CT (built around the rain)

10:00 AM — Culture: Bruce Museum, Greenwich, CT (~50 min from Millburn)
Indoor art, science & natural history museum — the "Six Extinctions" fossil exhibit is on now, plus the annual iCreate teen art show. A dry, engaging morning anchor.

12:00 PM — Lunch: Mediterraneo, Greenwich, CT (~50 min)
Bright, casual Mediterranean spot right on Greenwich Ave — hummus, wraps, salads. Easy with a kid at the table.

1:30 PM — City stroll: Greenwich Avenue, Greenwich, CT (~50 min)
Wide, tree-lined shopping street with awnings overhead — easy to duck into shops if the rain picks up.

3:00 PM — Dessert: Black Forest Pastry Shop, Greenwich, CT (~50 min)
Old-school European bakery known for its cakes, tortes and pastries. Warm, dry, sweet finish to the day.

Rough cost: ~$165 (museum admission + CT lunch/dessert pricing + parking + gas). A bit over target — the fossil exhibit and a genuinely new town make it worth it.

SUNDAY (Sep 13) — Delaware Water Gap / Blairstown, NJ (new territory, dry day earns the drive)

9:00 AM — Hike: Dunnfield Creek Trail, Worthington State Forest, Delaware Water Gap NRA, Columbia, NJ (~80 min)
Easy-to-moderate streamside walk along a cold mountain creek (part of the Appalachian Trail) — about 2.5–3 miles round trip with small cascades, no scrambling. This corner of NJ hasn't come up before; the drive is worth it for a real change of scenery.

11:00 AM — Other: Kittatinny Point Visitor Center & river overlook, Delaware Water Gap NRA, NJ (~80 min, same area — no extra drive)
Five-minute stop for the classic view of the Delaware River cutting through the ridge. Easy leg-stretcher after the trail.

12:30 PM — Lunch: Buck Hill Brewery and Restaurant, Blairstown, NJ — American (~65 min)
Warren County's first brewpub — burgers, wings, flatbreads, a laid-back patio.

2:00 PM — City stroll: Main Street Historic District, Blairstown, NJ (~65 min)
Small, tidy historic village center — 19th-century storefronts, a green, a few shops to poke into.

3:00 PM — Dessert: Blairstown Dairy, Blairstown, NJ — soft serve & sundaes (~65 min)
Old-fashioned ice cream stand — sundaes, shakes, waffle bowls.

Rough cost: ~$120 (state forest is low-cost, brewpub lunch, ice cream, gas).
